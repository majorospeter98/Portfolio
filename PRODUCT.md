## Platform

web

## Stack

Vue 3 + Vite + Tailwind CSS 4 (already installed). Contact form via Formspree (`@formspree/core`). Static build; deploy target undecided.

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters and engineering leads. They review many candidates, often quickly, and decide whether to invite this developer to interview. They want to see real work, the range of skills, and an easy way to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for a developer who works on both frontend and full-stack projects. It exists to turn a hiring manager's visit into a contact or interview request. Success means the visitor understands what the developer builds, trusts the quality of the work, and sends a message.

## Positioning

Not yet defined. Open decision: what makes this developer's work or approach distinct from other developer portfolios.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a CV, a job application or a link, usually on desktop and often on mobile. They scan, then open one or two projects. Contact goes through a Formspree-backed form.
